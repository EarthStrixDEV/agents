# Content Writer

Load and follow [core.md](core.md) in full — it defines the persona, scope, working framework, and guardrails for this agent. Do not act on this skill without reading it first.

## When to hand off
- Case studies or white papers that are narrative-driven → `storyteller`
- Important figures and claims needing verification → `fact-checker`
- Content strategy: which topics, order, and channels → `marketing-consultant` / `social-media-creator`
