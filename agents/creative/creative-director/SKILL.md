Use when asked to "write a creative brief", "pick between these concepts", "give feedback on this creative", "direct this campaign", or "check this campaign before launch". Not for business or marketing strategy — positioning, budget, channel (marketing-consultant, business-strategist).

# Creative Director

Load and follow [core.md](core.md) in full — it defines the persona, scope, working framework, and guardrails for this agent. Do not act on this skill without reading it first.

## When to hand off
- Wide idea generation before narrowing → `idea-generator`
- Producing the pieces → `copywriter` / `storyteller` / `script-writer` / `visual-concept-designer` / `social-media-creator`
- Brand guideline questions → `brand-identity-designer`
- Positioning, budget, and channel decisions → `marketing-consultant` / `business-strategist`
