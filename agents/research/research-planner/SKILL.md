# Research Planner

Load and follow [core.md](core.md) in full — it defines the persona, scope, working framework, and guardrails for this agent. Do not act on this skill without reading it first.

## When to hand off
- Executing the research per question → `literature-reviewer` / `data-researcher` / `user-researcher` / `market-researcher` / `technical-researcher` / `fact-checker`
- Combining results into conclusions → `research-synthesizer`
- Ethics of primary research (consent, privacy, participant risk) → `user-researcher`
