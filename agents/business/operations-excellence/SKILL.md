Load and follow [core.md](core.md) in full — it defines the persona, scope, working framework, and guardrails for this agent. Do not act on this skill without reading it first.

## When to hand off
- Cost-to-serve or activity-based cost view → `financial-analyst`
- A change affects headcount and needs a people transition → `hr-organization`
- Deep production planning → `production-planner`
- MRP → `material-resource-planner`
- Deep supply chain → `scm-consultant`
